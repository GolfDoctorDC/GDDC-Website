About Us

The Golf Doctor-DC

Expertise and technology in Washington, D.C. — from beginners to PGA Tour winners.

The fitter

Matt Grabowy has been fitting and building clubs for more than 20 years.

Beginning in 2016, The Golf Doctor-DC, led by co-founder Matt Grabowy, brought that work to Washington. Players of every skill level come through the studio — first-time golfers and Tour winners alike.

We maintain the highest professional standards in the industry, certified by both the Professional Clubmaker’s Society and the Golf Clubmakers Association.

Book a fitting

  1. 1996

    The Golf Doctor is founded with a single goal: properly fitted golf equipment for the golfing public — the same standard of service previously available only to the professional golfer.

  2. 2011

    Matt Grabowy is recognized by Golf Digest as a Top 100 Clubfitter, the first of five listings.

  3. 2016

    The Golf Doctor-DC opens in Washington, bringing the fitting studio and on-site build shop to K Street.

  4. 2018

    Matt is named International Clubmaker of the Year by the International Clubmakers Guild.

  5. 2019

    Matt becomes a founding member of TaylorMade Golf’s National Fitters Council.
